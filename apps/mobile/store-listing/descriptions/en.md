## Full description  (Play limit: 4000 characters)

YarnLog is a calm, offline companion for knitters and crocheters. Build your pattern, hit start, and it keeps your place — row by row, repeat by repeat — so both hands stay free for the needles.

No account. No ads. No internet required. Your projects live on your device and nowhere else.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Row-by-row counter — hold to advance, so a stray tap (or a curious cat) never loses your place.
• Build any project — break it into parts, sequences and rows, set repeats, and loop sections for socks and tubes.
• Reusable library — save rows, sequences and whole patterns once, then drop them into any future project.
• 30 built-in stitches for knit and crochet — plus define your own custom stitches, with chart symbols and notes.
• See repeats and progress at a glance, with a clear stitch chart for the current row.

MADE TO FEEL COZY
• Warm, handmade design that's easy on the eyes.
• Light, dark and automatic themes — dark mode shifts to a deep warm red for late-night knit-alongs.
• Available in English, Čeština and Deutsch.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. No sign-up, no tracking, no ads — ever. (Optional cloud backup may arrive in a future update; it will always be optional.)

Whether you're casting on your first scarf or juggling a cabled sweater across three parts, YarnLog keeps your count so you can enjoy the making.
