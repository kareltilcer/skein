# Target audience & content — recommended answers

Play Console ▸ App content ▸ **Target audience and content**

YarnLog is a general-interest craft tool for adult hobbyists. It is **not** designed for or
directed at children. Keeping children out of the target audience avoids the extra obligations
of Google Play's **Families** policy and the "designed for families" requirements.

## Target age groups

Select **adult** groups only — do **not** include any under-13 bracket:

- ☐ Ages 5 & under
- ☐ Ages 6–8
- ☐ Ages 9–12
- ☑ Ages 13–15  *(optional — teens do knit; include only if you want teen reach)*
- ☑ Ages 16–17  *(optional)*
- ☑ Ages 18 and over  ✅ **recommended primary**

**Simplest safe choice:** select **only "18 and over."** If you want broader reach, adding 13–17
is fine — just never tick an under-13 box, which would pull the app into the Families program.

## Appeal to children

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Could your app's store listing / content unintentionally appeal to children? | **No** — the visuals, copy and subject matter are aimed at adult crafters. |
| Is your app designed for families / children? | **No** |

Because no child age group is selected, Play will **not** ask you to join the Designed for
Families program and will not require the extra child-safety attestations.

## Store presence

- The listing will surface to the selected age groups.
- No "Teacher Approved" / Kids tab participation.

## Rationale

- The app collects no data (see `data-safety.md`), so even if teens use it there is no
  children's-data (COPPA/GDPR-K) exposure — but declaring an adult audience keeps the
  compliance surface minimal and matches the product intent.
