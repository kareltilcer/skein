# Ads declaration — recommended answer

Play Console ▸ App content ▸ **Ads**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app contain ads? | **No** |

YarnLog has no ad SDKs, no banner/interstitial/rewarded ads, no native ads, and no
affiliate/referral ad units. There are no ad-network libraries in the project.

With **No** selected, the "Contains ads" badge will **not** appear next to the app on Google Play.

> If this ever changes (e.g. a future ad-supported tier), update this answer — a mismatch
> between the declaration and actual behavior is a policy violation.
